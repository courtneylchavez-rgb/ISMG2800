About Me

Hi! My name is Courtney. In my free time, I like doing things that let me have fun and be creative. Some of my favorite hobbies are playing video games, watching horror movies, paddleboarding, and making crafts. I enjoy having a mix of relaxing activities and things that keep me busy and entertained.

I especially like horror movies because I enjoy being scared and watching interesting stories unfold. Video games are another favorite because I can explore different worlds and challenge myself. When I want to spend time outside, I enjoy paddleboarding, and crafts give me a chance to make something creative.
